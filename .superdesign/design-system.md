# Iron Sam — Design Direction

## Product

Iron Sam is a bilingual sports-fashion storefront for men and women in Libya. The first visual target is the customer home/storefront surface, designed mobile-first and expanded for tablet and desktop.

## Direction

Minimal editorial sportswear: confident typography, generous breathing room, crisp product presentation, and restrained UI chrome. The interface should feel premium and practical without locking the brand into final colors yet.

## Color guidance

Use neutral placeholders only for this first exploration: warm off-white or light neutral canvas, near-black type, soft gray surfaces, and a single temporary accent that is easy to replace. Do not establish a final brand palette.

## Typography

Use a clean sans-serif system stack with strong display hierarchy. Arabic must use a legible Arabic-capable sans-serif and mirror layout direction correctly. Keep labels short and scannable.

## Components

Cards should be simple and image-led. Buttons should have clear primary/secondary contrast. Product tiles need image, category/gender context, bilingual name support, LYD price, and availability state. Include an obvious cart badge and low-friction language switcher.

## Responsive behavior

Mobile-first single-column sections with horizontal category/product rails where useful. Desktop uses a centered max-width container, wider product grid, and persistent top navigation. Arabic uses true RTL layout; English uses LTR.

## Constraints

No online payment, accounts, reviews, wishlist, or complex animation in this first pass. Keep the visual system reusable for product detail, cart, checkout, and admin surfaces later.
